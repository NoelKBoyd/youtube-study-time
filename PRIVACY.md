# Privacy Policy: Study Time for YouTube

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Study Time for YouTube is a Chrome extension. It does not have accounts, servers or analytics, and the developer does not receive any of your data.

## What is stored on your device

The extension saves the following in your browser's local extension storage:

- your study sessions: topic, start and end time, planned and actual minutes, and whether the session was completed or stopped
- your goals (daily, weekly and per-topic targets)
- the YouTube Data API key you enter in Settings
- cached search results, kept for 24 hours
- your last topic and session length, to pre-fill the start form

While you are on YouTube, a list of videos you have already watched in that tab is kept in that tab's session storage and is removed when the tab closes.

None of this is sent to the developer or to any third party except as described below.

## What is sent to Google

This extension uses YouTube API Services. When a study session loads the video feed, your browser sends the topic you typed and your own API key to the YouTube Data API (googleapis.com) to get matching videos. Google handles that request under the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

By using this extension you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms

Video thumbnails are loaded from YouTube's image servers (i.ytimg.com) by your browser, as they are when you use YouTube normally.

## What the developer collects

Nothing. The extension has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no remote code. Your data is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose other than running the extension's study sessions, stats and goals.

The use of user data by this extension complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Deleting your data

- Delete one session or all sessions from the stats page (Delete, or Clear history).
- Remove cached results with Clear saved results in Settings.
- Remove your API key by clearing the field in Settings and saving.
- Uninstalling the extension removes everything it stored.
- You can export your session history as JSON from the stats page. This only happens when you click Export.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date above is updated. If the extension ever starts sending data anywhere new, the policy and the extension's store listing will be updated first.
